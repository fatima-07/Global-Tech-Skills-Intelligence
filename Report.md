### 📊 KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) for Your Report
These KPIs make your analysis look professional and measurable.

1. Skill Demand Index (SDI)
Percentage of job postings requiring a specific skill.

2. Skill Growth Rate (SGR)
Year-over-year increase in demand for a skill.

3. Developer Adoption Rate (DAR)
Percentage of developers using a skill (from surveys).

4. Training Enrollment Rate (TER)
How many learners are enrolling in courses for each skill.

5. Skill Popularity Gap (SPG)
Difference between:

what developers want to learn

what employers want to hire

6. Tool Usage Frequency (TUF)
How often IDEs, databases, or cloud tools appear in datasets.

7. Market Relevance Score (MRS)
Composite score combining:

job demand

developer usage

training interest

8. Salary Impact Index (SII)
Average salary difference when a skill is present in a job posting.
